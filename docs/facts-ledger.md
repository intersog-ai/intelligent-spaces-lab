## Corrections that matter

1. **Voice Preview Edition is $69 in the US, not $59.** €59 is the euro price.
2. **AI HAT+ 2 price: say "launched at $130, now $200".** The $50 April 2026 memory-driven rise is documented. The last $20 has no official note.
3. **HA 2026.8 did not "remove the Advanced mode toggle"** according to its own release post. It removed advanced/expert *wording*. The toggle's retirement is a separate developer-blog deprecation (May 2026).
4. **Matter cameras: only SmartThings and Home Assistant (2026.11, Nov 4, 2026) ship support.** Apple Home does not (as of iOS 27). I could not confirm Google Home or Alexa support.
5. **Piper is now GPL-3.0** (OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl). The MIT rhasspy/piper repo was archived Oct 6, 2025.
6. **SIA's "open-source" DC-09 Java library is PolyForm Noncommercial**, with a paid licence for commercial use. Do not call it open source for products.
7. **Greengrass V1 support has already ended** (Oct 7, 2026). Use past tense.
8. **MCP spec is 2026-07-28**, not 2025-06-18 or 2025-11-25, and it changes how version negotiation works.
9. **Hailo-10H LLMs are 1-2B models at about 5-10 tokens/s.** Do not imply 4B+ models or fast chat on the AI HAT+ 2.
